Terms of use

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Stridian is a university sports talent-profiling tool used by the university's sports coaches and students. By signing in you agree to these terms. How your information is handled is set out in the privacy policy.

Who can use it

  • Students of the university, who sign in with their university email and join a sport with the enrolment code their coach gives them.
  • Coaches and administrators, who sign in with an account made for them.
  • Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else has used your account, tell the administrator.

Using it fairly

  • Enter only true details about yourself, and only upload certificates, photos and match cards you are entitled to share.
  • Don't try to see or change anyone else's record, get around the limits on your account, or disrupt the service.

What its results are

Position suggestions, training plans and diet plans are guidance worked out from the information entered. They are not medical or dietetic advice, and they are not selection decisions — those stay with the coaches. Check with a qualified professional before making changes to training or diet.

Availability

Stridian is provided as it is, and it may change, or be unavailable at times. An account that breaks these terms may be suspended.
