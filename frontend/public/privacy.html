SStridian

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Stridian is a university sports talent-profiling tool. Students sign in and enter their details, coaches record test results and match or drill videos, and Stridian suggests the position that suits each student best, with the reasons, a training plan and a diet plan. This page explains what it stores and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Details a student enters: name, sport, age, height, weight, blood group, the position they play, diet preference, allergies, daily training hours and notes.
  • What coaches record: test results, notes, the position they confirm, and videos of drills and matches.
  • Student accounts: the student's university email address and a securely hashed password chosen for Stridian. Stridian never asks for, and never sees, the password of a university email account.
  • Coach accounts: name, email address, sport and a securely hashed password.

Email

To confirm that a university email address belongs to the person signing up, and to reset a forgotten password, Stridian emails a one-time 6-digit code to that address. That is the only email it sends. Only a scrambled (hashed) copy of the code is kept, and it stops working after 15 minutes.

How it is used

Only to produce each student's position analysis, training suggestions and diet plan, and to improve Stridian's position suggestions from coaches' confirmations. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside the university's coaching staff.

Who can see it

  • A student sees only their own record. Their report — position analysis, training and diet plan — appears once a coach has verified them, and they cannot change it. Coaches' private notes are not shown to students.
  • A coach sees only the students of the sport they coach.
  • The site administrator can see all students, including in a private Google Sheet summary.

Where it is stored

  • Student and coach records and accounts: a Postgres database hosted by Neon (Singapore).
  • Sign-in codes are sent through Google's Gmail service.
  • Videos and preview images: a private folder in the administrator's Google Drive. Video files are deleted 30 days after upload; the measurements taken from them are kept.
  • The website itself runs on Vercel.

Google account access

Stridian connects to the administrator's own Google account with the drive.file permission only. That lets it create and manage the video folder and the squad sheet it made itself — it cannot see any other file in that Drive. Stridian does not ask students or coaches to sign in with Google.

Stridian's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Ask your coach or the administrator, and your record, results and videos will be deleted. Deleting a student in Stridian removes their videos from storage as well. To have your sign-in account removed too, email the address below from your university email.
