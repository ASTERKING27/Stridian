SStridian

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Stridian is a university sports talent-profiling tool. Students sign in and enter their details, coaches record test results and match or drill videos, and Stridian suggests the position that suits each student best, with the reasons, a training plan and a diet plan. This page explains what it stores and who can see it.

What we collect

  • A student's record with the university's sports directorate: name, RA number, date of birth, mobile number, personal email, parents' names and mobile numbers, Aadhaar number, passport number (if any), identification mark, blood group, the highest level they have played at, and a photo.
  • Sport details a student enters: height, weight, the position they play, diet preference, allergies, daily training hours and notes.
  • Achievements: certificates a student uploads (photos or PDFs) as proof, what each one is for, and whether a coach verified or rejected it and why.
  • What coaches record: test results, notes, the position they confirm, and videos of drills and matches.
  • Student accounts: the student's university email address and a securely hashed password chosen for Stridian. Stridian never asks for, and never sees, the password of a university email account.
  • Coach accounts: name, email address, employee ID, mobile number, designation, sport and a securely hashed password.

Email

To confirm that a university email address belongs to the person signing up, and to reset a forgotten password, Stridian emails a one-time 6-digit code to that address. That is the only email it sends. Only a scrambled (hashed) copy of the code is kept, and it stops working after 15 minutes.

Reading certificates with AI

When a certificate is uploaded, Stridian sends that one image or PDF to Google's Gemini AI service, which reads the event, level, year and result off it so the student doesn't have to type them. The student checks those details before sending them to their coach, and a coach looks at the certificate itself before verifying it. Only the certificate is sent — never the student's Aadhaar, phone numbers or other details. Stridian currently uses Gemini's free service, under which Google may use what it is sent to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. If that is not acceptable for a certificate, ask the administrator to add it for you instead.

How it is used

Only to produce each student's position analysis, training suggestions and diet plan, and to improve Stridian's position suggestions from coaches' confirmations. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside the university's coaching staff.

Who can see it

  • A student sees only their own record. They can correct their own details, except that their RA number, sport and date of birth, once given, are changed only by an administrator. Their report — position analysis, training and diet plan — appears once a coach has verified them, and they cannot change it. Coaches' private notes are not shown to students.
  • A coach sees only the students of the sport they coach — their full record, Aadhaar and passport numbers included — and can download it as an Excel file.
  • The site administrators can see and change every student's record, in every sport, including in private Google Sheets kept in the administrator's Google Drive.

Where it is stored

  • Student and coach records and accounts: a Postgres database hosted by Neon (Singapore).
  • Sign-in codes are sent through Google's Gmail service.
  • Videos, preview images, photos and certificates: a private folder in the administrator's Google Drive. Video files are deleted 30 days after upload; the measurements taken from them are kept. Photos and certificates are kept until the student's record is deleted.
  • The website itself runs on Vercel.

Visitor statistics

Stridian uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits to the site. It sets no cookies and records nothing that identifies you — only the page, the time, the site you came from, your approximate location (country or city), and your type of device and browser. Visits are told apart by a scrambled code that is discarded after 24 hours. Nothing you type into Stridian is sent to it.

Google account access

Stridian connects to the administrator's own Google account with the drive.file permission only. That lets it create and manage the folder and the spreadsheets it made itself — it cannot see any other file in that Drive. Stridian does not ask students or coaches to sign in with Google.

Stridian's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Ask your coach or the administrator, and your record, results, videos, photo and certificates will be deleted. Deleting a student in Stridian removes those files from storage as well. To have your sign-in account removed too, email the address below from your university email.
