05 · BUILDING ITNotes for whoever implements this
WHO GETS THISiPhone 8 and later (every iPhone on iOS 16+): CHHapticEngine.capabilitiesForHardware().supportsHaptics. Android 12+ where vibrator.areAllPrimitivesSupported(TICK, CLICK, LOW_TICK, THUD, QUICK_RISE, SLOW_RISE, QUICK_FALL) is true. Everyone else gets the budget plan.
IPHONEOne AHAP file per signature moment (launch, level, badge, streak restored). Start the haptic engine when the app starts — before the splash — so the first spark isn’t late. Call prepare() on the feedback generators on touch-down.
ANDROIDPrimitives play one after another, so the sparks and the charge run in sequence instead of layered. Read the real primitive lengths with getPrimitiveDurations() and time each one to end where the animation expects. Use performHapticFeedback for taps and ticks — it follows the phone’s Touch feedback setting.
SYNCStart every pattern on the same frame as its animation, from the same timeline numbers. Target under 10 ms drift.
ONE CHANGELevel reached: start the count-up at 1400 ms instead of 1250 so its ticks don’t land on top of the letter stamps.
REDUCE MOTIONHaptics go quiet with the animations: no Crackle, Charge, Dive, Sweep, Stamp, Draw or Rise. Taps, ticks, switches and Success / Warning / Error stay. The launch becomes a single Land when the app appears.
SETTINGSAdd Settings › Haptics: Full · Light · Off. Light keeps taps and results only. Off means nothing plays, whatever the phone can do.
