CASE ACan vary strength hasVibrator() · hasAmplitudeControl() = true · composition primitives missingWaveforms with amplitudes. Short spikes feel like sparks, and 60 ms steps fake a rise.
CASE BOn / off motor only hasVibrator() = true · hasAmplitudeControl() = falseTimings only. Few, short, well-spaced pulses — the rhythm carries the feeling, not the strength.
CASE CNo motor, or haptics off hasVibrator() = false · or Settings › Haptics is OffNothing plays. Every haptic already has a visual twin, so nothing is lost.
02 · OPENING THE APP · 0 – 2800 MSThe launch, rebuilt from pulses
On / off: three spark pulses, a 60 ms thump when the logo fires, then four pulses closing in (250 → 90 ms apart) so the zoom still feels like it’s speeding up, ending on a 45 ms land. 300 ms of vibration in total. With strength control the sparks, charge and dive come back as spikes and 60 ms steps.

05 · RULES FOR CHEAP MOTORSWhy the budget plan looks the way it does
SHORTEST PULSE20–30 ms. Anything shorter often isn’t felt on a cheap motor — so selection ticks mostly disappear, on purpose.
GAPSAt least 80 ms between pulses. Closer than that and a spinning-weight motor smears them into one buzz.
LIMITSNo pulse over 80 ms. At most 6 pulses and 400 ms of vibration in one moment. Never loop, never during loading or uploads.
ONE AT A TIMECancel the pattern that’s playing before starting a new one — cheap motors can’t mix.
SYSTEM FIRSTPrefer VIRTUAL_KEY, CLOCK_TICK, CONFIRM and REJECT through performHapticFeedback. Each phone maker tunes these to its own motor, and they follow the Touch feedback setting.
WHAT’S CUTCrackle becomes 3 pulses. Charge, Draw, Rise, Fall, Sweep and Stamp are dropped. Slider steps, scrub stills and outline ticks are dropped. Level reached keeps two moments: the logo pop and the finish.
IPHONENo budget iPhones to plan for: every iPhone that runs iOS 16 or later has the full haptic engine.
WEB BUILDIf Stridian ships as the current website: iPhone browsers can’t vibrate at all, and Android Chrome only does on / off (navigator.vibrate). That makes Case B the ceiling on the web — the rich plan needs the native app.
